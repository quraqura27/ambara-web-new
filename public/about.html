Our Story

Built for Clear Cargo Coordination

PT Ambara Artha Globaltrans was founded in 2025 to help businesses plan air freight and Indonesia cargo movements with clearer shipment data, document preparation, and operational handoffs.

Our CGK operating focus supports coordination with airlines, warehouses, ground handlers, customs-service partners, and delivery providers according to each shipment's requirements.

Our mission is to give shippers practical next steps, transparent scope, and careful coordination for cargo moving to or from Indonesia.
