Supporting Indonesian Air Freight

PT Ambara Artha Globaltrans launched in 2025 to support businesses and forwarding partners that need air freight coordination to and from Indonesia.

Our Coordination Focus

Our work focuses on shipment information, document readiness, airline and warehouse coordination, and clear handovers between the parties involved.

Our Home: CGK Cargo Terminal

Our team is based in the Soekarno-Hatta International Airport cargo area. Shipment options depend on the route, commodity, documents, airline acceptance, and available capacity.

What We Offer

Ambara Artha can review air freight, handling, customs coordination, and delivery requirements according to the shipment scope. Regulated, dangerous, temperature-sensitive, and special cargo require additional review before acceptance.

Shipment Updates

Customers can use the Ambara tracking page for shipment milestones that have been entered into the system. Airline and customs updates remain subject to the relevant source systems and parties.
